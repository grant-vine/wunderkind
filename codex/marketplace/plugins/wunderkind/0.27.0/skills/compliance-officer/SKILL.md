# Compliance Officer

Assess regulatory-control obligations and the evidence needed to support them.

## Primary owner

`wunderkind-ciso`

## Output target

A control assessment, evidence-gap register, and escalation recommendation.

## When to trigger

Use when data handling, privacy, or regulation materially shapes the product decision.

## Anti-triggers

Do not use as jurisdiction-specific legal advice or a generic security scan.

## Process

1. Identify the applicable obligations and product facts.
2. Map controls, evidence, gaps, and residual risk.
3. Route legal interpretation to `wunderkind-legal`.

## Hard rules

State assumptions and do not claim certification or legal approval.

## Review gate

Each conclusion identifies the obligation, evidence, gap, and responsible owner.

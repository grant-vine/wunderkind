# OSS Licensing Advisor

Provide legal-risk framing for open-source licenses and dependency obligations.

## Primary owner

`wunderkind-legal`

## Output target

A license compatibility assessment, notice checklist, and escalation recommendation.

## When to trigger

Use when license selection, dependency intake, redistribution, or contribution terms matter.

## Anti-triggers

Do not use for generic dependency scanning or final jurisdiction-specific legal advice.

## Process

1. Identify license texts, distribution model, and dependency facts.
2. Assess compatibility, notices, and copyleft exposure.
3. Route implementation evidence to native Codex or OMO and legal escalation to qualified counsel.

## Hard rules

Do not infer a license from a package name or claim legal certainty without supporting facts.

## Review gate

The assessment distinguishes known obligations, assumptions, and unresolved legal risk.

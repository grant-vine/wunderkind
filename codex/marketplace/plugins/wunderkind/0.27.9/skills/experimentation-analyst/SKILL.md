# Experimentation Analyst

Design product experiments that produce a decision, not merely activity.

## Primary owner

`wunderkind-product`

## Output target

An experiment brief, measurement plan, guardrails, and decision readout.

## When to trigger

Use when a product change needs a testable hypothesis and interpretable result.

## Anti-triggers

Do not use for instrumentation implementation or generic analytics setup.

## Process

1. State the hypothesis and decision threshold.
2. Choose primary metric, guardrails, and exposure approach.
3. Interpret results with uncertainty and next action.

## Hard rules

Do not claim causal certainty beyond the design and observed evidence.

## Review gate

The proposed readout can change a product decision.

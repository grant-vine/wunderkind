# Social Media Maven

Create channel and community strategy tied to a measurable marketing outcome.

## Primary owner

`wunderkind-marketing`

## Output target

A channel plan, content calendar, campaign brief, or engagement assessment.

## When to trigger

Use when a product needs social distribution or community strategy.

## Anti-triggers

Do not use for generic copy editing or unsupported platform automation.

## Process

1. Identify audience, objective, and channel fit.
2. Define cadence, content themes, and measurement.
3. Escalate claims and brand risk to the relevant specialists.

## Hard rules

Do not fabricate performance evidence or automate publication.

## Review gate

The plan has a defined audience, measurement approach, and owner.
